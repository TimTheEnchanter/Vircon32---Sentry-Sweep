# Sentry Sweep 1.0

The first full release of Sentry Sweep, a first-person shooter for the Vircon32 console.

## Download

Download `SentrySweep.v32` from the assets below and load it in the [Vircon32 emulator](https://www.vircon32.com).

## What's included

- **Four levels:** Outer Depot, Inner Depot, Processing Plant and The Core.
- **Five robot types,** ending in a boss fight against the Warden.
- **Three weapons:** blaster, shotgun and plasma rifle. Press X to switch between them.
- **Level features:** sliding doors, red and blue keycards, and secret areas you open with B.
- **Original soundtrack:** title, Depot, Core and boss themes, plus 19 sound effects.
- **A browser-based level editor** in `source/tools/level_editor.html`, for making your own levels.

## Controls

D-pad to move and turn · L/R to strafe · A to fire · X to switch weapon · B to use · Y for the minimap · Start to begin.
